# Analysis

## Layer 3, Head 1

I think this is a better example of paying attention to the tokens that come after 
the current token compared to layer 3 head 10 except for row [CLS].

Example Sentences:
- She carefully and quietly [MASK] the door to avoid waking anyone.
- The playful [MASK] chased the fluffy, bouncing ball outside.

## Layer 2, Head 7

It seems to pay attention to the nouns of the sentence. This includes the adjectives
that describe the noun as well. I find that this works better for the first sentence 
than it does the second one though.

Example Sentences:
- She carefully and quietly [MASK] the door to avoid waking anyone.
- The playful [MASK] chased the fluffy, bouncing ball outside.
